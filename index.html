Key Clarifications

Frequently Asked Questions

View all FAQs →

Does DCI guarantee challan cancellation or insurance claim approval?

No. DCI provides structured legal guidance, documentation review, and permitted escalation support. Final outcomes depend on competent authorities, courts, or insurers.

Is DCI membership mandatory when buying a vehicle at a dealership?

No. Customer enrolment at any dealership is completely optional and requires explicit customer consent. Vehicle sales cannot be conditioned on purchasing a DCI membership.

How do I activate and download my Digital Membership Certificate?

Once your payment is verified by the payment gateway and consent is recorded, your unique Membership ID is generated immediately and available in your Customer Dashboard.
