Legal Disclosures & Platform Policies

Terms & Conditions, Privacy Policy, Refund & Cancellation Policy, and Compliance Disclaimers.

1. Compliance & Non-Guarantee Disclaimer

Drive Chambers India (DCI) is an independent private membership platform. DCI does not represent any government authority, traffic police department, court, motor insurer, or statutory regulator. Membership does not confer immunity from traffic laws and does not guarantee challan cancellation, penalty reduction, or insurance claim approval.

Advocate referrals and legal consultations respect professional independence and the customer's absolute right to choose their own legal representation.

2. Terms & Conditions

Memberships are valid for 12 months from server-verified activation for the specific enrolled vehicle. Historical memberships retain the plan version, pricing, inclusions, and exclusions accepted at the time of purchase. Dealership enrolment is strictly optional and requires explicit customer consent.

3. Privacy & Data Governance Policy

DCI collects only data necessary for membership issuance, vehicle registration, and support case delivery. Sensitive KYC, policy, and claim documents are stored in private role-restricted storage with expiring download links and are not exposed to sales users. Marketing consent is kept separate from essential service notifications.

4. Refund & Cancellation Policy

Payments are processed via RBI-compliant payment gateways. Duplicate or failed payments are automatically reconciled and refunded. Cancellation and refund requests on active memberships are governed by the approved cancellation policy, with full transaction and audit records maintained in the portal.
