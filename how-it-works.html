Operational Workflow

From Dealership Onboarding to Customer Case Resolution

Every step in the DCI ecosystem is governed by role-based permissions, explicit customer consent, and immutable audit logs.

End-to-End Hierarchy & Enrolment Flow

A

Super Admin Creates DDS & Assigns Territory

Super Admin creates the District Director of Sales (DDS) account and assigns a specific district (e.g. Meerut). DDS sees only their assigned district.

B

DDS Registers Dealership

DDS submits dealership legal/trade details, OEM brand, category (2W/4W/Multi), GSTIN, and KYC documents. Status starts as Pending Admin Verification.

C

Admin Verifies Dealership

Super Admin verifies documents and chooses Approve, Reject (reason mandatory), Request Correction, or Suspend. Manager onboarding unlocks only after approval.

D

Dealership Manager Onboards Sales Agents

Approved dealerships activate a Dealership Manager, who invites and manages Sales Agents (activate/deactivate subject to audit).

E

Sales Agent Registers Customer & Sells Plan

Agent records customer & vehicle details, displays plan price/exclusions, captures explicit non-pre-ticked consent, and sends a payment link. Membership activates only after server-verified payment.

F

Customer Logs In & Accesses Support

Customer logs in via OTP/credentials to view their Digital Membership Card, download their PDF certificate, raise support cases, and track case timelines.
