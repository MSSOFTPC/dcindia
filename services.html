Support Modules

Structured Assistance & Case Management

Every active member can raise cases, upload documents securely, and track progress through our 6-stage case lifecycle.

Module 01

Traffic Challan Assistance

Submit challan details, notice/photo uploads, state/city, and query description. Our team performs an initial review, eligibility check, procedural guidance, and permitted referral or escalation with live status updates.

Customer Inputs:

• Challan number & notice photo

• State / City & query summary

Note: No guarantee of challan cancellation or penalty discount.

Module 02

Motor Insurance Claim Assistance

Facing a claim rejection or unexplained deduction? Upload your policy, claim number, and rejection/deduction letters for structured document review, grievance guidance, insurer escalation support, and eligible advocate referral.

Customer Inputs:

• Insurer, Policy & Claim Number

• Rejection / deduction documents

Note: Claim approval remains solely with the insurer/ombudsman.

Module 03

Professional Legal Assistance

Submit your matter category, summary, supporting documents, urgency, and preferred callback time. We conduct a qualified professional review, scope check, and consultation scheduling while safeguarding your independent choice of advocate.

Internal Workflow:

• Qualified review & scope check

• Consultation scheduling & record

Subject to plan consultation limits and exclusions.

Module 04 • Non-Emergency Guidance

Road Accident Guidance

Log incident date, time, location, injury/property details, and police/insurance references to receive a structured documentation checklist, support triage, and permitted referral.

Emergency Notice: This platform is NOT an emergency response service. In case of immediate danger, injury, or medical emergency, please contact local emergency services (112 / 100 / 108) immediately.

Customer Inputs:

• Incident date, time & location

• Police / FIR & insurance reference

• Supporting photos/uploads
